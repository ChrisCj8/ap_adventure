apAdventure is a GMod gamemode that uses the Archipelago randomizer to create randomized campaigns out of player-made configs.

Setup Instructions can be found [here](./apworld/docs/setup_en.md).

Custom content for this gamemode can be shared on [it's discord server](https://discord.gg/K9TcW8r5mw). I'm not super fond of people having to join a discord for this, so I'm gonna look into finding a better way to let people share their stuff in the future, but this at least gives people some way to share their stuff for now.
